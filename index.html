Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Maoku Tool ("the Tool", "we", "us") handles information when you use our desktop application. The Tool is a personal, local automation utility that publishes videos and uploads subtitle (caption) files to the user's own YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API.

1. Information We Collect

The Tool operates locally on your computer. It processes the following data in order to provide its functionality:

  • Local media files: video files and subtitle (.srt) files located in your own local folders, used only to upload them to the YouTube channel you authorize.
  • YouTube channel metadata: video titles, video IDs and caption track information of your own channel, obtained through the YouTube Data API to match files correctly and avoid duplicate uploads.
  • Authentication tokens: OAuth credentials stored locally to access the YouTube Data API on your behalf. These never leave your computer except for the standard OAuth authorization flow with Google.

We do not collect names, email addresses, browsing history, location, device identifiers, or any analytics data.

2. How We Use Information

  • To upload the videos and subtitles you prepare to the YouTube channel you authorize.
  • To check existing caption tracks so that duplicate uploads are avoided.
  • To maintain the operation and security of the Tool.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with any third party, except as required by law. The only external interaction is with Google's YouTube Data API, which receives the data you explicitly instruct the Tool to send (for example, a subtitle file you choose to upload). Your interaction with Google services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data processed by the Tool remains on your local device. OAuth tokens are stored in local credential files and are protected by your operating system's user account security. You can revoke the Tool's access at any time through your Google Account settings (Security > Third-party access).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Local files remain on your device until you delete them. We do not operate any remote servers, so there is no server-side data to retain. To request deletion of any data, or to remove all traces of the Tool's access: (1) revoke access in your Google Account settings (Security > Third-party access > Maoku Tool > Remove access), and (2) delete the local credential files. Contact us at the address below and we will confirm the deletion.

6. Children's Privacy

The Tool is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page with an updated effective date.
